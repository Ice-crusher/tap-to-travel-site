# Privacy Policy for Tap To Travel

**Last updated:** September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Tap To Travel** (the “App”) processes information when you install and use it.

## 1. Who is responsible for the App?

The App is developed and operated by **Andrii Romanets** (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

For privacy questions or requests, use the support contact shown on the Tap To Travel Steam store page. Do not include passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## 2. Information processed by the App

### Background keyboard and mouse events

While the App is running, it observes keyboard press/release events and mouse-button press events to increment your in-game steps. On operating systems that require permission, monitoring operates only when that permission has been granted.

The App does **not** record or transmit:

- the text you type;
- the content of your clicks;
- cursor coordinates;
- the names or contents of other applications or windows; or
- a history of individual keyboard or mouse events.

A key code may be held briefly in memory to prevent a held key from being counted repeatedly. It is discarded when the key is released. On macOS, this feature requires Accessibility permission. You can refuse or revoke this permission in macOS System Settings, although background step counting will then be unavailable. Closing the App stops its input monitoring.

### Approximate initial location and weather

When no saved avatar position is available, the App may use your public IP address to determine an approximate starting location. The App requests current weather information from WeatherAPI. WeatherAPI receives your IP address as part of the network connection and, for the initial lookup, uses it to return an approximate location.

If that lookup fails, the App may request your public IP address from ipify. The App then compares the address locally against an included DB-IP country database. Your IP address is not sent to DB-IP by the App.

When weather is refreshed later, the App sends the avatar’s current map coordinates to WeatherAPI. These coordinates represent the avatar’s in-game position and are not GPS location data collected from your device.

### Map requests

The App downloads map styles, tiles, fonts, and related map resources from OpenFreeMap. As with ordinary internet connections, the provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, user agent, request time, and requested resource.

### Gameplay data

The App stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

- avatar position and active destination;
- step and coin totals;
- inventory, cosmetics, equipped items, and saved outfits;
- visited countries, landmarks, and passport progress;
- treasure and booster timers;
- App settings and preferences; and
- locally mirrored Steam statistics and achievement progress.

### Steam services

When Steam is available, the App uses Steamworks for entitlement checks, achievements, statistics, overlay features, and Steam Cloud. Gameplay data may be stored in Steam Cloud as `walkdemo-save-v1.json` and associated by Valve with your Steam account.

Valve processes Steam account and platform information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your Steam password or payment-card information.

### Logs and diagnostics

The App may create local runtime or diagnostic logs containing technical events, errors, weather-request purposes, avatar coordinates, weather results, or Steam status information. The App does not automatically send these logs to us. If you choose to send diagnostic information when requesting support, we will process what you provide to investigate the problem.

## 3. Why information is processed

We process information to:

- provide background step counting and other App features you request;
- save and synchronize gameplay progress;
- select an initial in-game location and provide location-based weather;
- display maps and map resources;
- provide Steam achievements and statistics;
- maintain security, prevent abuse, and diagnose technical problems; and
- respond to support and privacy requests.

For users in the European Economic Area, the main legal bases are performance of the contract or steps taken at your request to provide the App, and our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the App. Where the law requires consent for a particular optional feature, you may refuse or withdraw that consent without affecting unrelated App features.

## 4. Service providers and recipients

The App may communicate with:

- [Valve/Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/) — distribution, entitlement, achievements, statistics, overlay, and optional cloud saves;
- [WeatherAPI](https://www.weatherapi.com/privacy.aspx) — initial approximate location and weather information;
- [ipify](https://www.ipify.org/) — fallback retrieval of your public IP address;
- [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org/) — map styles, tiles, fonts, and related resources; and
- [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) — hosting this Privacy Policy through GitHub Pages.

These providers may process technical information under their own terms and privacy policies. Some providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area, subject to the safeguards applicable to the provider and transfer.

We do not sell personal information. The current version of the App does not include advertising, third-party advertising trackers, or developer-operated behavioural analytics.

## 5. Storage and retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you clear it, uninstall the App, or remove the relevant application-data files. Some operating systems may preserve application data after uninstalling.

Steam Cloud data remains subject to your Steam Cloud settings and Valve’s retention practices. You can disable Steam Cloud through Steam. Where the App provides a cloud-save deletion control, you may also use that control to request deletion of the App’s cloud-save file.

We retain support communications and information you send us only for as long as reasonably necessary to answer the request, resolve disputes, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. External providers determine the retention periods for information they process independently.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can:

- decline or revoke macOS Accessibility permission;
- stop background step counting by closing the App, or by revoking Accessibility permission on macOS;
- disable Steam Cloud in Steam;
- clear local App data and saved gameplay information; and
- stop further processing by uninstalling the App, subject to information retained by Steam or other providers.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection concerning your personal data. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. In Poland, the supervisory authority is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Because most gameplay data is kept locally or by Steam rather than in a developer-operated account, we may not possess or be able to identify that data. We may ask for enough information to verify and handle a request, but you should never send us your Steam password.

## 7. Children

The App is not designed to collect names, contact details, or other directly identifying information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us so that we can investigate and delete it where appropriate. Parents and guardians should review Steam’s family and age-related settings.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the information handled by the App. No software, device, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, provide additional notice through the App or Steam.

## 10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the support contact shown on the Tap To Travel Steam store page. Do not include passwords or other unnecessary sensitive information.
